## 1. Quantitative Evaluation Matrix

| Evaluation Dimension | Weight | Score (1–10) | Evaluation Notes / Justification |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Problem Clarity & Relevance** | 10% | `/10` | Is the problem real, urgent, and well-understood? |
| **2. Real User Impact** | 10% | `/10` | Does this save meaningful time, money, or cognitive load? |
| **3. Innovation & Novelty** | 10% | `/10` | Is this a fresh insight or just another CRUD wrapper? |
| **4. Technical Depth & Complexity** | 10% | `/10` | Is the engineering non-trivial, modular, and defensible? |
| **5. AI Quality & Guardrails** | 10% | `/10` | Is AI used meaningfully with strict validation and fallbacks? |
| **6. UI / UX Quality & Polish** | 10% | `/10` | Is the interface intuitive, responsive, and aesthetically sharp? |
| **7. Reliability & Error Resilience** | 10% | `/10` | Does the app handle edge cases, empty states, and errors? |
| **8. Scalability & Architecture** | 10% | `/10` | Can this architecture scale beyond a toy demo? |
| **9. Demo Execution & Storytelling** | 10% | `/10` | Is the demo under 3 mins, crisp, and high-energy? |
| **10. Competitive Differentiation** | 10% | `/10` | Why will this win against 50 other teams? |
