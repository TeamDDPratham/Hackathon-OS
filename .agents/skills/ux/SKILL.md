# UX & Interaction Design Skill

## Purpose
This skill ensures the product feels exceptionally polished, communicates its purpose within 30 seconds, and minimizes cognitive friction during evaluation.

## UX Design Principles
1. **The 30-Second Rule**:
   - A first-time user looking at the landing/app screen must instantly understand:
     1. What this product does.
     2. Who it is for.
     3. What action to take first (one clear, high-contrast primary CTA).
2. **Visual Hierarchy & Contrast**:
   - Clear size differentiation between headings ($H1 > H2 > H3$), body text, and metadata labels.
   - Primary action buttons must visually dominate secondary/ghost buttons.
3. **Immediate Feedback & State Transitions**:
   - Provide instant tactile feedback on click (disabled state during processing, micro-animations).
   - Use toast notifications or subtle status badges for async events.
4. **Frictionless Onboarding**:
   - Offer a "Try Sample Data" or "Demo Mode" one-click button so judges can experience the product without typing extensive inputs.
